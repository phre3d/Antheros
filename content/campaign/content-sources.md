# Modules Used

While the overall backdrop is the [[settlements/haven/free-city-of-haven|Free City of Haven]], the campaign plot draws from many sources. Most are purchased modules but some are homebrew ( *in italics* ).  The key plot source items are listed below in order started - some overlap in duration.
