# Diplomat's Pack

>[!info]
>**Rarity:** Common
>
>**Weight:** 25 lbs + contents<br>
>**Dimensions:** <br>
>**Base Price:** 39 gp

A basic chest that can hold 12 cubic feet of gear weighing up to 300 lbs. It comes with:

- 2 cases for maps and scrolls
- a set of fine clothes
- a bottle of ink
- an ink pen
- a lamp
- 2 flasks of oil
- 5 sheets of paper
- a vial of perfume
- sealing wax
- and soap
