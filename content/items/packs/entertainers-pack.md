# Entertainer's Pack

>[!info]
>**Rarity:** Common
>
>**Weight:** 5 lbs + contents<br>
>**Dimensions:** <br>
>**Base Price:** 40 gp

A basic backpack that can hold 1 cubic foot of gear weighing up to 30 lbs. Additional items may be strapped to the outside. It comes with:

- a bedroll
- 2 costumes
- 5 candles
- 5 days of rations
- a waterskin
- a disguise kit.
