>**Duration:** 7 days

## Description

A ruby tonic. It smells like herbs, but tastes like strawberries.

## Effects

When imbibed ot creates a map showing the best path home in the imbiber's head. The path leads to the last official home by the imbiber, not just the birth home.

## History

This potion was created by a ranger who traveled the entire world, seeking out mysteries.
