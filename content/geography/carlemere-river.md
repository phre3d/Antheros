From its headwaters south of the Great Hawks, the Carlemere flows more or less southwest, passing south of the [[geography/khuz-mountains|Khuz Mountains]].  It joins the [[geography/dorian-river|Dorian River]] about 50 miles north of the [[settlements/haven/free-city-of-haven|Free City of Haven]].
