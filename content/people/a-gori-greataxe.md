# General Info

A small be-spectacled dwarf who runs a small print shop in [[settlements/haven/the-outlands/the-dwarrow|The Dwarrow]], and publishes a weekly newsletter featuring local news and gossip called The TeaSeR. Most of the TeaSeR's readers buy it for the well-drawn cartoons contributed by various local artists; nonetheless, Gori uses the newsletter as a vehicle to pontificate about his pet peeve, the absence of bearded dwarven women in Haven. Gori swears that there is a dwarven city near the borders of the Namori Empire where such bearded beauties dwell, and has actively been trying to organize an expedition to bring back some as wives.

Most of the older dwarves view these claims with skepticism, but Gori has developed quite a following among the younger males of [[settlements/haven/the-outlands/the-dwarrow|The Dwarrow]].

 It is also rumored that [[people/browin-treebeard|Browin Treebeard]], the proprietor of [[landmarks/martin-metals|Martin Metals]] and a regular advertiser on the back page of the TeaSeR, has donated a substantial sum of capital to finance the expedition.
