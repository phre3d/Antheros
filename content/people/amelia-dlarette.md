# General Info

Bartholomew's wife is a plump and matronly woman, is a lover of music and theater, having grown up on [[settlements/haven/the-south-corridor/the-street-of-harps|The Street of Harps]]. She is the daughter of [[people/artemis-guisto|Artemis Guisto]], the Drum maker and has five children - Branden, Ophelia, Andrio, Carmen, and Petrio.

 She is involved more in running the inn than in direct contact with the guests. She spends a good deal of her time in the kitchen gossiping with [[people/brisla-stoneaxe|Brisla Stoneaxe]] the cook and knows everyone on [[settlements/haven/the-south-corridor/the-street-of-harps|The Street of Harps]].
