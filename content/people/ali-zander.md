There is a passenger aboard Atlantia, a handsome young man, dark-haired and bearded, dressed as a merchant. He is actually a mage entrusted with the delivery of certain letters and documents from the [[organizations/professional/mages-guild|Mage's Guild]] to various high- ranking mages in Belakor.
