# General Info

Bing and his brother, [[people/bob|Bob]], seem to act as two halves of one personality. He has a ruddy-face and slightly rotund and is a bit taller than [[people/bob|Bob]].

 Bing fancies himself a singer and loves to sing love songs and ballads, particularly when there are ladies present. [[people/bob|Bob]] may be known for his interest in the opposite sex, but Bing always seems to get the girl.

 He and [[people/bob|Bob]] stick together, and frequently one finishes a sentence the other had started, and they occasionally answer in unison.

 Bing uses a sling with proficiency but will fight only to save his or [[people/bob|Bob]]'s life; content to take cover under a wagon at any hint of trouble.

 The brothers are also the cooks for the troupe.
