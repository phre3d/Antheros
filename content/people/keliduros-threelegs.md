# General Info

One of Baraggio's old traveling companions, a centaur who walks using a peg-leg after being maimed in a fight with bandits on the last of Baraggio's major expeditions to the distant east.

 He is a sharp bargainer and very strong in will (he's virtually unbluffable). He can generally be found at the warehouse in the mornings and early afternoons, but prefers to spend his evenings among his own kind near the [[settlements/haven/the-south-corridor/horse-market|Horse Market]], and at night will normally be at [[landmarks/the-arrows-flight|The Arrow's Flight]], a centaur tavern next to the trolley yard.
