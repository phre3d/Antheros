# General Info

The night assistant at [[landmarks/the-golden-ducat|The Golden Ducat]], Harlequin Bussbender, is a youth of 20, blond-haired and blue-eyed. He works a variety of jobs between 4:00pm and 2:00am (the official closing hour for the inn), tending the front desk, serving meals and drinks, and answering guest demands for services or repairs. He is sometimes lackadaisical in performing his duties, which earns him regular tongue-lashings from Perkins.

The son of an affluent mercantile family, he dreams of someday running off with a beautiful noblewoman; he has excellent skills with thrown missile weapons, and an instinctive knack for repairing mechanical implements.
