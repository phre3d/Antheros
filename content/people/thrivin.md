# General Info

A moneylender, who will make loans of up to 20,000gp to a dwarf from [[settlements/haven/the-outlands/the-dwarrow|The Dwarrow]], up to 10,000gp to other residents of Haven, and up to 5000gp to non-residents. His interest rates range from 5-20% per week, depending on the collateral a borrower has to put up.
