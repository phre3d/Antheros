# General Info

A slight, grey haired avanthar of many years' experience in the caravan trade. Wing (as he is usually called) tends to frequent the northern route on the expeditions he organizes, and has many business connections. Occasionally he will guide a caravan to the east onto the Golden Plateau, a large and very rich region populated by bands of roving nomads.  

 He captains caravans from Haven, but maintains only a sleeping space there in the home of one of the other avanthari; his family resides in the crags and cliffs of the White Wing Mountains near Seki, southeast of Haven. Wing will fly home whenever he has sufficient free time to warrant the trip.

 Usually, Wing leaves the actual organizing of the caravans (mostly horseback) to his assistant, a centaur named [[people/turikonis-plainsrunner|Turikonis Plainsrunner]] (#1716), who does the majority of the hiring. Current plans call for the pair to leave within a week on their next venture.
