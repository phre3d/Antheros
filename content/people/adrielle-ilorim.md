# General Info

Daughter of [[people/firlirrel-ilorim|Firlirrel Ilorim]] and [[people/lachoriel-ilorim|Lachoriel Ilorim]], Adrielle is a lovely young elven woman of middle height, with long black hair. She appears frequently to be totally absorbed in her own thoughts, even when dealing with customers.

 Adrielle disappeared for a number of years when still a very young girl of 30, but eventually returned, and now runs a shop ([[landmarks/fine-silks-and-fabrics|Fine Silks and Fabrics]]) on Lorien Street in [[settlements/haven/the-outlands/erinhir|Erinhir]] specializing in silks and other fine fabrics.

 She somewhat secretly funds the informal watch group calling themselves [[settlements/haven/legal-system/adrielles-watch|Adrielle's Watch]]. How she comes by the money is a mystery.

Adrielle's shop has been under the scrutiny of the [[organizations/criminal/thieves-guild|Thieves' Guild]] (as the object of a probable robbery) for some time. As a result of this, the guild has noticed that Adrielle has a second "business" - trade in human flesh.  Refer to scenario [[plots/free-city-of-haven/scenarios/silks-and-slavery|Silks and Slavery]].
