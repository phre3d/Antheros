# General Info

One of [[people/sulemon-duur|Sulemon Duur]]'s most trusted lieutenants, Jurgan (#1827) oversees one of the larger chapters of the Guild, although not the most wealthy. Jurgan is a suspicious individual, and does not allow thieves to come to his home, but meets with only his journeymen in one of several taverns, allowing them to pass directives down through the ranks.
