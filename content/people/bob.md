# General Info

Bob and his brother, [[people/bing|Bing]], seem to act as two halves of one personality. He is ruddy-faced, slightly rotund and losing his hair.

 Bob tells jokes and funny stories, and has a quick wit, and an incredible arsenal of bad puns. He is also known for his interest in the opposite sex, but [[people/bing|Bing]] always seems to get the girl.

 He and [[people/bing|Bing]] stick together, and frequently one finishes a sentence the other had started, and they occasionally answer in unison.

 Bob uses a sling with proficiency but will fight only to save his or [[people/bing|Bing]]'s life; content to take cover under a wagon at any hint of trouble.

 The brothers are also the cooks for the troupe.
