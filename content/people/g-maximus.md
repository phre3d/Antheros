# General Info

The huge, obese, pig-eyed owner of [[landmarks/the-black-whip|The Black Whip]], a house of pleasure on [[settlements/haven/the-labyrinth/the-street-of-silk-veils|The Street of Silk Veils]] which caters to those who enjoy any of a variety of strange and bizarre fetishes.

Maximus lives at his somewhat disreputable establishment, and controls three cells in [[organizations/criminal/angelinas-guild|Angelina's Guild]] containing some dozen men. One group consists of the three or four thugs in his employ, another of the uniquely trained staff of the Whip, and the third a group of four men who handle Black Hand involvement in the Haven slave trade - the leader of these men, Angus McFarland, is [[people/adrielle-ilorim|Adrielle Ilorim]]'s contact in [[settlements/haven/the-outlands/erinhir|Erinhir]].

Maximus himself is as venal and loathsome as the activities he directs, and is well satisfied with his grimy little niche in the overall [[organizations/criminal/the-black-hand|The Black Hand]] power structure. There are few things beneath him, and it is rumored that he partakes liberally of the services offered by his establishment.
