# General Info

Tolbin is a broad-shouldered, robust dwarf with a well-kept beard and thinning hair. He bought [[landmarks/the-stone-hammer|The Stone Hammer]] five years ago, after thirty years as a mercenary and adventurer. He still maintains his fighting skills with a few hours of training each week at the salle d'armes of [[people/eldri|Eldri]] the weapons teacher, and is not a man to be trifled with in the event of a barroom altercation. Tolbin himself yearns more than occasionally for his old lifestyle, and plans on joining the party that accompanies the re-forged Broken Blade back to Arkendelve.

 Tolbin is the de facto 2nd in command of the [[organizations/political/brotherhood-of-the-broken-blade|Brotherhood of the Broken Blade]]. Tolbin is perhaps the most persuasive orator of the group, and is often the one chosen to convince a recalcitrant candidate to test his destiny. For his own part, he is certain that [[people/nithri-griffinrider|Nithri Griffinrider]], the reclusive dwarven warrior who refuses to touch the blade, is the chosen one, and the two often engage in vociferous arguments at [[landmarks/the-stone-hammer|The Stone Hammer]].
