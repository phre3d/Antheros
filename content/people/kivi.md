# General Info

A talented youth, Kivi and her partner [[people/petriko|Petriko]] spend their days teaching at a small dance and acrobatic school which they own, near [[settlements/haven/the-commons/the-commons|The Commons]]. Their incredible abilities have drawn many affluent clients to their small school.

 At night, they perform at various points along [[settlements/haven/the-south-corridor/the-street-of-harps|The Street of Harps]], the Plaza of Troubadours, as well as the Leiderstrasse. They have several times been employed to perform at houses in [[settlements/haven/the-heights/the-heights|The Heights]], and their popularity and fame is steadily increasing.

 Kivi and [[people/petriko|Petriko]] are inseparable, yet they are not married, and do not seem to plan to do so.

 Kivi is an incredibly beautiful and alluring girl with blond, straight hair, bright green eyes, and elfin features. Her grace and agility is no less than phenomenal.
