# General Info

A tall, loosely made man who appears unfinished. The nickname has been given him by the guards and prisoners at [[landmarks/the-grey-halls|The Grey Halls]], and he does deserve it; a low intelligence and a tendency to overt sadism would usually get one fired, but Recci's luck has functioned beautifully. Recci attained the position of watch chief through attrition rather than merit.

 He has never been caught out of line, nor has any fault or misstep been traced to him - [[people/turin-khaey|Turin Khaey]] suspects, but as yet has not sufficient evidence to do anything.

 Recci hangs out at [[landmarks/the-knighte-oute|The Knighte Oute]] when off duty, and would likely (85%) succumb to a bribe if one were offered to him to free a prisoner.

 He's not smart enough to double-cross anybody, and there is reasonable chance for a successful rescue, with his cooperation.
