# General Info

Corwin is master of one of [[organizations/criminal/the-black-hand|The Black Hand]]'s two traditional thieving guilds. He is an older, more cautious man, who has been with the Hand for many years. Because of his experience and his known trustworthiness, Ariosto has been assigned the much more difficult territory of [[settlements/haven/the-south-corridor/the-south-corridor|The South Corridor]] and adjoining areas including [[settlements/haven/the-labyrinth/the-labyrinth|The Labyrinth]].

 His guild is larger, containing four cells (almost twenty men) and includes a cell made up of the finest Hand thieves - possibly the best thieving team in Haven, although a few individuals surpass them in skill.
