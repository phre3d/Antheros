# General Info

A sandy-haired, handsome young halfling who frequently takes long trips out of Haven, the purpose of which he remains silent about. On the rare occasions he returns to [[settlements/haven/the-outlands/the-dell|The Dell]], he is usually surrounded and pressed for tales of his travels.

 He may also be cajoled into playing a few tunes on the fiddle, which always brings a crowd to [[landmarks/the-green|The Green]]. Barleyman is quite an experienced thief, but will not ply his trade while within the confines of the city.
