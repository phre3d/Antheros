# General Info

Paraggio, the master of one of [[organizations/criminal/the-black-hand|The Black Hand]]'s two traditional thieving guilds is a slender, boyish man with dark hair and an angular face. His guild consists of some three cells of highly competent thieves who operate mostly in [[settlements/haven/the-commons/the-commons|The Commons]] and North Corridor.
