# General Info

Fagin, a wiry old half-elf with wild eyes, has set up a thriving business in child labor, capturing "street rats" in the twisting alleyways and dark corners of [[settlements/haven/the-labyrinth/the-labyrinth|The Labyrinth]]. The ragged children, along with the occasional careless tiefling, are starved and beaten into submission, and sold for slave (indenture) labor in the workhouses of [[settlements/haven/the-labyrinth/the-labyrinth|The Labyrinth]] or [[settlements/haven/the-commons/the-commons|The Commons]]
