# General Info

Round as a dumpling, with rosy cheeks and bright eyes, and perpetually wearing a floured apron. Pippin sells hot pies and pastries from his pushcart on [[settlements/haven/trade-island/trade-island|Trade Island]] (where the prices are higher) baked fresh every night in [[settlements/haven/the-outlands/the-dell|The Dell]]. The cart has an enchanted [[people/brick|Brick]] in it to keep the goodies just-out-of-the-oven hot,

 Price Range: High
