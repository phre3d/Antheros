# General Info

Meriwether, a small man hunched from years of leaning over his work, usually has time to talk unless his mouth is full of tacks. He does know a good deal of gossip about the area, and the comings and goings of its residents, and can be an invaluable source of information (both the [[organizations/criminal/thieves-guild|Thieves' Guild]] and [[organizations/criminal/the-black-hand|The Black Hand]] use him for news of the North Corridor).
