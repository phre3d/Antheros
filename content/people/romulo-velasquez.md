# General Info

Of all the artists receiving [[people/gilberto-maggia|Gilberto Maggia]]'s support, Velasquez is the most favored; he has a small private studio on [[landmarks/studio-lane|Studio Lane]], and receives a generous monthly stipend.

 Velasquez is tall and dark, with craggy features that are striking though not handsome; he considers himself quite a Lothario, and has indeed had his way with a number of young, aristocratic maidens. He has attempted to ply his charms with [[people/marita-maggia|Marita Maggia]], in the hopes of securing a more permanent place in the Maggia household, but received nothing but cold shoulder (he remembers these encounters with more than a little bitterness, and would like to find a way to take some wind out of Marita's sails).

 On two previous occasions, he has been approached by agents of [[people/prospero-maggia|Prospero Maggia]], and asked to garner information on a particular business transaction being contemplated by Gilberto. Romulo considers such efforts an easy way to increase his income, and has no moral qualms about spying on his benefactor.
