# General Info

Short and getting stout in his later years, Damiano has a long beard, and his chestnut hair is shot with grey. Damiano represents a number of Dwarven communities in the south-eastern mountains, and his cart contains toys and other small well-crafted items made by skilled Dwarven craftsmen abroad and in Haven, often carrying mail and other messages to and from [[settlements/haven/the-outlands/the-dwarrow|The Dwarrow]].

 Damiano speaks the [[languages/dwarven-language|Dwarven Language]] well, and has a 20% bonus to dealings with Dwarves. It is said that he is never (well, hardly ever) cheated by a Dwarf, because he is also a sharp bargainer,

 Damiano is well-acquainted with [[settlements/haven/the-outlands/the-dwarrow|The Dwarrow]], and knows of both the [[organizations/political/brotherhood-of-the-broken-blade|Brotherhood of the Broken Blade]], and the planned expedition by [[people/a-gori-greataxe|A. Gori Greataxe]] (see [[settlements/haven/the-outlands/the-dwarrow|The Dwarrow]], Scenarios).
