# General Info

Husband of [[people/valma-geronde|Valma]] (nee [[organizations/families/geronde-family|Geronde]]), Hanzal is a leatherworker from a merchant background who repairs tack and saddles in a little shop located very close to the city's immense [[settlements/haven/the-south-corridor/horse-market|Horse Market]]. He and Valma live with the rest of the Matters family in a crowded, but nicely maintained, two-story wood structure in the residential area west of [[settlements/haven/the-south-corridor/the-street-of-harps|The Street of Harps]].
