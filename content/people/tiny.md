# General Info

The proprietor of [[landmarks/the-crystal-ship|The Crystal Ship]] is an immense, ham-fisted fellow known as Tiny who is capable of keeping order even among seamen. Although he is not a formal member of [[organizations/criminal/the-black-hand|The Black Hand]], he is connected with them through the opium trade, and is not foolish enough to risk everything in any plot against them.
