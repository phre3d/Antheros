# General Info

A spare, dark bearded dwarf with a sharp tongue and a ready wit, Kobri operates a small cobbler shop located near the northernmost end of [[settlements/haven/the-south-corridor/magic-street|Magic Street]], at the very edge of [[settlements/haven/the-outlands/the-dwarrow|The Dwarrow]]. Kobri found a unique, and to most of his dwarven peers repulsive, solution to the problem of finding a spouse; he married a goblin woman, a very young apprentice mage named [[people/zbyssso|Zbyssso]].

 His marriage has thus far been a happy one, despite the constant taunts that he must endure from his neighbors. Indeed, he has not succumbed to the social pressures of the community at all, and visits freely around [[settlements/haven/the-outlands/the-dwarrow|The Dwarrow]] both with and without his bride.

 He wears a bracelet of semi-precious stones intertwined among strands of his love's hair; if he is threatened on the street, he will wield the bracelet as if it were a magical weapon (thus far, fortunately for Kobri, nobody has called his bluff).

 He is friendly to any who ask his assistance, and offers freely of his hearth and home - needless to say, however, those that accept such hospitality do not exactly move to the top of the dwarven hit parade.
