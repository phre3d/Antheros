# General Info

Morgan is close friends with, and serves with [[people/clemente-panfusille|Clemente Panfusille]] as officers in Haven's prestigious [[organizations/fighting/citadel-guard|Citadel Guard]]. Both have served in the Guard for more than ten years, and are very pleased to have the chance to exert authority over the vendors and visitors of [[settlements/haven/trade-island/trade-island|Trade Island]].  

 Morgan is tall but slightly built, with a plain face adorned by a well-kept van dyke and bushy sideburns. He is much more comfortable with his career than with his home life, for he has great difficulty dealing with his son's physical deformity; he consequently spends most of his time at his Citadel duties.

 He is a very loyal husband to [[people/astrilde-de-la-riis|Astrilde de la Riis]], uninterested in other women, and happy with his rather pedestrian duties harboring no great urges to seek a more adventurous avocation.
