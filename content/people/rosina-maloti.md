# General Info

Rosina is the best of several seamstresses in Haven who specialize in costumes for the stage. A matronly lady with spectacles, she is an expert in costuming history, and can tell a good deal about a person by the way they dress. Rosina has a 30% bonus to penetrating a disguise, and can create authentic garb for almost any period in time and place.
