# Citadel Guard

This brightly garbed, and well-armored unit is the only formal military unit in the City. While technically a regiment, their strength is more that of a platoon with about 20 troops. The Guard is the historical protectors of the sanctity of [[settlements/haven/trade-island/trade-island|Trade Island]] from a time when the region was a wilderness backwater in the former empire.

In addition to guarding [[landmarks/the-citadel|The Citadel]], they also patrol Trade Island and act as a local Watch. And though they are funded by the city, the Guard is not part of the [[settlements/haven/legal-system/haven-city-watch|Haven City Watch]].
