# Art of Enhancement

>[!info]
>**Parent Class:** [[classes/physician/physician|Physician]]
>
> **Source:** Luis Tejada / Gyro (Dungeon Masters Guild)

 While the Art of Purification may be viewed with mild disdain, the Art of Enhancement is treated with outright scorn. To traditionalists, the Art of Enhancement is no Art at all, but a bastardization of everything a Physician should hold dear.  Students of this school use their Physic to improve their combat prowess, though whether or not they do this to help others is up to the Physician in question.

 Called 'war-surgeons' at best and 'quacks' at worst, these Physicians treat their enemies as diseases to be cured or tumors to be excised. Their emotional connection to Physic is most characterized by fanaticism or boisterous confidence. Most students of this Art are beholden to no school. In fact, Enhancement is the second most common art to develop spontaneously, with Restoration being the most common.

##  Operating Theatre of War  

 Beginning when you select this art at 2nd level, you gain proficiency with light armor and with one type of melee weapon of your choice.

##  Doping  

 Starting at 2nd level, you develop a forbidden method of supplementing your performance. Provided that you aren't wearing medium or heavy armor, or equipped with a shield, you are able to use Succor to improve your physical parameters. You can use your bonus action and a use of Succor to start doping for the next minute. This effect ends early if you don medium or heavy armor, or if you choose to dismiss it. Ending it early does not require an action.

 While you are doping, you gain the following effects:

 - You gain a bonus to your AC equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1).
 - You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.
 - You suspend the effects of Exhaustion for the duration of this feature.
 - You gain a bonus to any Constitution saving throws you make to maintain concentration on a spell. The bonus equals your Charisma modifier (minimum of +1).

##  Extra Attack  

 At 6th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

##  Bloodbath  

 Starting at 10th level, the Succor you infuse during your doping starts to react with the flow of battle. When you make a successful melee attack against a creature while doping, you regain a fourth of the damage dealt in hit points (rounded up).

##  Between Life and Death  

 At 14th level, your doping nullifies your sense of pain, allowing you to ignore otherwise fatal wounds. If you drop to 0 hit points while doping and don't die outright, you can make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. If you succeed, you drop to 1 hit point instead. Each time you use this feature after the first, the DC increases by 5. When you finish a short or long rest, the DC resets to 10.

##  Physician-Assisted Homicide  

 Starting at 18th level, you refuse to let anything come between you and a new target. When you bring a creature to 0 hit points while doping, you can choose another creature within 30 feet. As a bonus action, you can make an attack roll against them. If you are successful, you deal damage as normal and appear in the closest unoccupied space to them. Moving in this way ignores attacks of opportunity and difficult terrain.
