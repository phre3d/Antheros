A green-brown herb.

# Natural Range

Desert


# Common Use(s)

> [!author-tip]
> Consider how it's use - more or less as-is, or as an ingredient

Consuming this herb will protect against scorpion and spider stings for a period of three days, granting advantage on Constitution saving throws against poison from arachnids.

Used in crafting as a source of [[materials/essence-of-shadow | Essence of Shadow ]].


# Distribution
## Trade & Market

Can be found for sale by herbalists or in magic/alchemical supply shops. Occasionally, a potion maker may be persuaded to part with some but it will be with a 20%-50% markup.
