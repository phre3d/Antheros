A smallish, evergreen bush resembling the dwarf holly, but produces pale gray berries instead of the typical bright red berries of the holly. 

# Natural Range

Arctic and Mountains


# Common Use(s)

> [!author-tip]
> Consider how it's use - more or less as-is, or as an ingredient

These berries can be juiced to create a strong paralytic poison that can be applied to a slashing or piercing weapon. A target damaged by the weapon must make a Wisdom saving throw or is slowed as if by the slow spell. Additionally, the creature must make a Constitution saving throw at the end of each of it's turns for 4 rounds. On a failed save, the target is paralyzed for 1 minute. The DC for both the Wisdom and Constitution saving throws is equal to 8 + the Nature skill of the person who prepared the poison. The potency of this poison dissipates after a successful strike.

Used in crafting as a source of [[materials/essence-of-shadow | Essence of Shadow ]].


# Distribution
## Trade & Market

Can be found for sale by herbalists or in magic/alchemical supply shops. Occasionally, a potion maker may be persuaded to part with some but it will be with a 20%-50% markup.
