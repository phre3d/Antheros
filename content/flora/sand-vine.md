# Sand Vine

>[!info]
>IMAGE
>
>---
>
>**Value:** 4 gp  <br>
>**Rarity:** Uncommon <br>
>**Related Professions:** [[professions/herbalist|Herbalist]]<br>


A relatively rare rope-like seaweed found along temperate or warmer coasts, it grows both above and below water and is commonly rooted to a small rock. 

# Natural Range

Coastal


# Common Use(s)

> [!author-tip]
> Consider how it's use - more or less as-is, or as an ingredient

Can be dried and used as rope. 

Using the herbalism kit, its juices can be extracted to produce vine oil, a topical anesthetic granting 5 temporary hit points for 1 hour. 

- Has the side effect that within 24 hours of use, a creature cannot stabilize on its own (so even with successful death saves, the creature or person must be assisted). 

- Regular use gives a creature a signature odor of salt and slightly-rotted fish.

Used in crafting as a source of [[materials/essence-of-shadow | Essence of Shadow ]].


# Distribution
## Trade & Market

Can be found for sale by herbalists or in magic/alchemical supply shops. Occasionally, a potion maker may be persuaded to part with some but it will be with a 20%-50% markup.
