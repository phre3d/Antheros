# Darkberry

>[!info]
>IMAGE
>
>---
>
>**Value:** 26 gp  <br>
>**Rarity:** Rare <br>
>**Related Professions:** [[professions/herbalist|Herbalist]]<br>


Short bushes with spindly branches and fluffy green leaves, the plant is known for the unusual berries that only a few produce. The small purple berries contain essence from the Plane of Shadow, and the bushes are said to only grow where the Shadow Weave is strongest. 

# Natural Range

Forests and Underground


# Common Use(s)

> [!author-tip]
> Consider how it's use - more or less as-is, or as an ingredient

When a darkberry is split or crushed, it releases shadowstuff, creating a circle of magical darkness with a 5-foot radius sphere, lasting for 1 minute. 

Used in crafting as a source of [[materials/essence-of-shadow | Essence of Shadow ]].


# Distribution
## Trade & Market

Can be found for sale by herbalists or in magic/alchemical supply shops. Occasionally, a potion maker may be persuaded to part with some but it will be with a 20%-50% markup.
