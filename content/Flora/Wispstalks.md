# Wispstalks

>[!info]
>IMAGE
>
>---
>
>**Value:** 56 gp  <br>
>**Rarity:** Very Rare <br>
>**Related Professions:** [[professions/herbalist|Herbalist]]<br>


This rare fungus has become something of a fable among herbalists. It is reported to have a large, translucent blue bulbous cap growing atop a thin stem, and normally forms in small clusters deep within damp cave environments and forests. 

# Natural Range

Forests and Underground


# Common Use(s)

> [!author-tip]
> Consider how it's use - more or less as-is, or as an ingredient

When consumed, the creature must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 13). 
- On a failed save, the creature takes 1d6 poison damage and nothing else happens except the creature coughing up what part of the fungus they tried to consume. 
- On a success the creature becomes transparent (but not invisible) for 1 hour, gaining a +10 bonus to Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

Used in crafting as a source of [[materials/essence-of-air | Essence of Air ]].


# Distribution
## Trade & Market

Can be found for sale by herbalists or in magic/alchemical supply shops. Occasionally, a potion maker may be persuaded to part with some but it will be with a 20%-50% markup.
