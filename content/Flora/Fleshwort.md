# Fleshwort

>[!info]
>IMAGE
>
>---
>
>**Value:** 30 sp  <br>
>**Rarity:** Common <br>
>**Related Professions:** [[professions/herbalist|Herbalist]]<br>


A short plant with gray stalks and olive-colored hairy leaves, the fleshwort thrives in areas where corpses lay. Often the plant will be seen on the site of battlefields, or where the dead were quickly buried. 

# Natural Range

Grasslands and Underground


# Common Use(s)

> [!author-tip]
> Consider how it's use - more or less as-is, or as an ingredient

Some healers use the fibrous body of the fleshwort to speed up the healing process during a short rest (a character may add 1d6 to the total amount of hit points recovered).

Used in crafting as a source of [[materials/essence-of-shadow | Essence of Shadow ]].


# Distribution
## Trade & Market

Can be found for sale by herbalists or in magic/alchemical supply shops. Occasionally, a potion maker may be persuaded to part with some but it will be with a 20%-50% markup.
