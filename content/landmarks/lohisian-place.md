***Hospital of Worship***

## Description

[[religions/deities/lohi|Lohi]], Goddess of Healing isn't worshipped in the traditional manner but rather through acts of succor for the ill and injured and healing. This site at the east end of [[settlements/haven/the-north-corridor/believers-lane|Believer's Lane]] is therefore not a temple per se but rather three buildings that house varying specialties.
