## Description

The shipyard is a large, walled complex, with ways for building and dry-dock repair for three ships. This is the first yard that [[people/nimrodel|Nimrodel]] started with over 150 years ago, and he still maintains his primary offices here. He owns three other yards. One upriver, and two downriver, outside the walls, for major new building. Except for the office in one corner of the yard, the rest of the large yard is covered with scattered sheds and roofed over areas for the storage of spars, masts, lumber, kegs of pitch, etc. for shipbuilding. There are gates to the yard on both Warehouse Row and Drayer's Loop, which are locked at night, and patrolled by six private guards.

 [[people/nimrodel|Nimrodel]] employs nearly 100 laborers in his shipyard, involved in carpentry, ship fittings, and other similar skills. There are also a number of clerks, and ship designers. There is only a 5% chance of a job opening for qualified men, as [[people/nimrodel|Nimrodel]] is known as a fair and honest man, and men rarely leave his employ once hired on. [[people/nimrodel|Nimrodel]] himself does little save design work. And leaves most of the running of the yard to his son [[people/perendhil|Perendhil]], and the foreman, [[people/hanson-frigate|Hanson Frigate]].

 At present, two of the three ways are occupied. One is a new vessel, little more than a skeleton hull, and the other is one of the city's galleys, Dorian Queen, in dry-dock for minor refitting, including a new rudder.
