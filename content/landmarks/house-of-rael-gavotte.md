## Dwelling of the Guilder

## Description

A luxurious three floor house of fine stone, enclosed in a high stone wall, with an ornate bronze gate. The interior is well furnished and the gardens out back are well-tended, with beautiful statuary.  

 The studio (and living quarters) of [[people/gustav-morris|Gustav Morris]], a young energetic composer working under the patronage of the [[organizations/families/maggia-family|Maggia Family]]. Tall and thin, with curly blond hair (Gustav works odd hours, plunking on a harpsichord far into the night). Gustav has a well-deserved reputation for eccentricity, both in his person and his music.
