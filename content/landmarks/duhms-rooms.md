***A rooming house***

## Description

[[people/glumin-duhm|Glumin Duhm]] - is a morose man of middle years. He appears gray, and even projects an aura of gray. He will tell you all the terrible things that have happened to him, and the problem of his tenants, raining a veritable barrage of pessimism upon the ears of anyone willing to listen (or unwilling to be impolite and walk away).

## Exterior

A large rambling, two- to four-story brick-and-wood frame building, occupying nearly one quarter of a large city block. There are some ten different entrances, from both main streets and alleys. The building's wooden portion is painted a dull battleship gray.

## Interior

Over 150 different rooms and suites make up this incredible warren. Duhm himself has a three room suite on the first floor near the entrance at the corner of Loom and Broom.

## Hours

Duhm is usually available to rent rooms from 11:00am to 5:00pm.

## Price Range

Fair, from 12 SP per week for a 10' square furnished room with a communal bath and a communal kitchen, to 6gp per week for a four room suite with private bath and kitchen (a regular little apartment).

## Quality

Good. All the rooms are reasonably clean, and Duhm does not allow rats.

## Cash Box

All rents are due on the first day of the week, in advance, and Duhm makes a trip to the [[landmarks/house-of-hofstra|House of Hofstra]], a banking family in the financial district as soon as they are all collected. His weekly take would be about 450gp if the entire set is rented. Normally about 2/3 of the rooms are occupied.

## Staff

Duhm, that's it - only [[people/glumin-duhm|Glumin Duhm]].

## Customers

There is a 10% chance that there will be a tenant in Duhm's room, complaining about something.

## Loiterers

Who would loiter in a rooming house?

## NPC Encounters

## Outstanding Items

## Special Options

There are usually rooms and suites available. Since there are less of the suites, the chances of a vacancy should be about 25%, but there is always a furnished room available.
