***Home away from home for the sisterhood***

## Description

This is the local home of the Lydian Sisterhood, a guild open to all female mercenaries. They provide a hostel for sick and injured members that don't want to make use of these services at a temple of Strathos.

If one is looking to hire a mercenary or two "off the books", you might be able to do so here (or [[landmarks/free-companies-house|Free Companies House]]) but formal contracts are done via [[maps/haven/markers/summ-mercenaries|Summ Mercenaries]].
