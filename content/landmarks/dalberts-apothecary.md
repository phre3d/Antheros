## Description

[[people/dalbert|Dalbert]] runs a small, low budget Apothecary on Datashi Street, in [[settlements/haven/the-commons/the-commons|The Commons]]. The two halfling poisoners from [[landmarks/silvantes|Silvante's]] (Angelina's headquarters) buy the supplies necessary for their trade here. [[people/dalbert|Dalbert]] is affiliated with [[organizations/criminal/the-black-hand|The Black Hand]], and his shop also provides a convenient short term storage place for contraband opium and hashish.
