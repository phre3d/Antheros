# Sisters of the Unicorn

Members follow the teachings of the goddesses in the Pantheon (except for Laydi), placing their feminity above all.  This does not mean they are against the male gods, or men in general, nor does this impact their sexuality in any way - all women are welcome.

 **Symbol:**  White unicorn on a black background.
