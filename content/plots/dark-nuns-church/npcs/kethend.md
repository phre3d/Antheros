# Description 
Kethend is a 23-year-old female Dragonborn armorer.
- She has a bald head and red eyes.
- She has smooth, ghostly, white scales.
- She stands 182cm (5'11") tall and has a fat build.
- She has a diamond-shaped, bland face.
- She is albino.

# Personality 
- She discretely claims to worship [[religions/deities/thalos|Thalos]]: God of the Night, Patron of Thieves, Lord of Mischief
- In truth, she secretly worships [[religions/deities/ladros|Ladros]]: God of Knowledge, Father of the Gods. 
- She is incredibly conceited. 
- She works hard to play hard afterward.

# Plot Hook 
In the [[plots/dark-nuns-church/dark-nuns-church|Dark Nun's Church]] storyline she has been hired to steal items from the PCs.
