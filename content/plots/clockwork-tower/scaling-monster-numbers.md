# Scaling Monster Numbers

>[!info]
>![[plots/clockwork-tower/table-of-contents|Table of Contents]]

````col
```col-md
**Party Size** 

| Party Size | Increase number by |
|:-:|:-:|
| 5-8 | x 1.5 |
| 9-12 | x 2 |
| 13| x 2.5 |

```

```col-md 
 **Party Level** 

| Party Level | Increase number by |
|:-:|:-:|
| 5-8| x 1.5 |
| 9-12| x 2 |
| 13 | x 2.5 |
| 16+ | X 3.5 |

```
````

Use the tables above to determine adjustments for party size and for party level; then multiply these numbers together.

For instance, if you have a Party Size of 5 with an average Party Level of 10, then you would add 1.5 + 2.5 to get 4 (3.75 rounded up) times the monsters.

Three creatures for a level 10 party are not a challenge, especially if there are 6 or 7 PC's. Increase that to 12, and suddenly you have a challenge. Feel free to adjust as you see fit.

In addition to scaling monsters, many of the traps and puzzles in the game have a sliding scale that the DM can use to change the level of difficulty depending on the skillset of the characters.
