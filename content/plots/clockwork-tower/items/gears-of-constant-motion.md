# Gears of Constant Motion

>[!info] Image
>![[plots/clockwork-tower/images/gears-of-constant-motion.jpg]]

**Item Type:** Wondrous Item<br>
**Rarity:** Very Rare<br>
**Price:** <br>
**Weight:** <br>
**Attunement:** Yes

# Properties

- The wearer's speed increases by 10 feet.
- The wearer can move up vertical surfaces and across liquids without falling during the move.
- Once per long rest, the wearer can activate the boots to grant them an additional action on their turn.

# Description

These finely crafted boots have tiny, perpetually moving gears embedded into their soles and up their sides.
