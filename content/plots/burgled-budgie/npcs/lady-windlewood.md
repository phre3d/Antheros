# Lady ELizabeth Windlewood

>[!info] Portrait
>![[plots/burgled-budgie/images/elizabeth-windlewood.jpg]]
>*Used for Foundry* (AI created)
>![[plots/burgled-budgie/images/lady-windlewood.jpg]]

*Medium, human, Lawful Entitled*

Lady ELizabeth Windlewood is a petite elderly woman but moves with purpose. Her clothing, fashionable and costly, is complemented by jewels sparkling on her wrists, neck, and ears. Her hair is secured with an elaborate ornament, exposing her angular face and contributing to her austere appearance.

She is upset by the loss of her bird and expects the PCs to find Beatrice, and quickly!

- She gets fed up with too much questioning and carries herself with a haughty disposition towards anyone who is not nobility themselves (especially non-humans).
- She responds very well to flattery and deference when addressing her.
