# Gifted Ribbon

>[!info] Image

**Item Type:** Wondrous Item<br>

**Price:** <br>
**Weight:** <br>

# Properties

Up to three times a day, while directly helping or protecting an innocent, any roll of 1 for an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw can be rerolled. The subsequent roll must be used.

Appears as a faded, red, hair-ribbon.
