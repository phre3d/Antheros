# Alcohol

>[!info]
>**Type:** Organic<br>
>**Value:** Varies depending on specific formulation and intended use.<br>
>**Rarity:**
>
>**Odour:** Strong<br>
>**Taste:** Depends on the plant used<br>
>**Colour:** Depends on the plant used<br>
>**Common State:** Liquid<br>
>**Related Professions** [[professions/vintner|Vintner]], [[professions/herbalist|Herbalist]],  [[professions/brewer|Brewer]], [[professions/alchemist|Alchemist]]

A liquid formed but the fermentation of various plants. Two common forms are wine, made from various fruits (primarily grapes) and beer, made from different grains.

# Properties

## Physical Characteristics

Colour depends on the fermentation inputs. Strong scent.

## Properties

Highly flammable

## Origin

Created by [[professions/vintner|Vintners]], [[professions/brewer|Brewers]], and [[professions/alchemist|Alchemists]], depending on the intended use.

# Usage

## Common Use

The most common use is as an intoxicant to aid in having a good time or drowning one's sorrows.

Also used by [[professions/herbalist|Herbalists]] as a base for making  *common*  potions.

## Manufacturing / Products

Can be distilled to further increase the concentration, producing various products such as whisky, gin, vodka etc.
